### Connect Snowtime

1. In Snowtime, open **Settings → API keys** and create a key. Choose **write** to start and
   stop timers; a **read** key only shows your timer and entries.
2. Paste the key into **API Key**.
3. If your Snowtime runs at another address than `https://snowtime.snowhound.eu`, set
   **Instance URL** to it, such as `https://time.example.com`.

You can change both later under **Extensions → Snowtime** in Raycast's settings.
